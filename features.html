Everything Triple Peaks does

One coach. Six ways it shows up for you.

From the weekly plan to the race-day taper, Triple Peaks is designed around the actual decisions an endurance athlete makes every day.

Weekly planning

A plan for the week you're actually having.

Every Monday, Triple Peaks rebuilds your week around your current form, how last week actually went, and the hours you really have — balancing swim, bike, and run so the hard days don't collide.

  • Easy days stay easy, hard days earn the gains — the proven 80/20 split.
  • Volume scaled to the time you've got, with an honest week-over-week score.
Workout & week review

Every workout — and every week — reviewed, not just recorded.

When a session ends, Triple Peaks scores it against the plan: how you paced it, how hard it really was, what it means for tomorrow. Then every Sunday it sums the whole week up in plain English — what you completed, how it went, and what it sets up next.

  • A per-workout execution score: Excellent, Good, On Track, or Needs Improvement.
  • A weekly recap that names the sessions that actually moved you forward.
Structured workouts

Every detail, planned — interval by interval.

Key sessions come fully built — warm-up, main set, cool-down — each step with its own target pace, power, or heart rate. Send it to your Apple Watch and just follow along, no squinting at a plan mid-interval.

  • Every step has a target — pace, power, or heart-rate zone.
  • Push to Apple Watch for guided, step-by-step execution.
AI Coach

Ask your coach anything — with context.

It already knows your plan, your last four weeks, and your check-ins. Ask it to reshuffle the week, swap a session, or pressure-test your race plan — and get a straight answer, not a hedge.

  • Sees your full picture: fitness, fatigue, sleep, and recent sessions.
  • Suggests changes you can apply to your plan in one tap.
Race periodization

From 5K to Ironman, built backwards from race day.

Set your goal race and Triple Peaks maps the whole season — Base, Build, Peak, then taper — with the right build-up and race-specific work for your distance.

  • The full season at a glance, with weeks-to-go and your current phase.
  • Tuned to your race: Sprint, Olympic, 70.3, Ironman, marathon, or ultra.

Strava & Apple Health sync

Connect once. Runs, rides, swims, and multi-sport all flow in automatically — GPS, heart rate, pace, and power, with no duplicates.

Daily check-in

Tap Good, Tired, or Unwell. Add a sleep score 1–5 and a note. Your coach factors it in before today's recommendations.
