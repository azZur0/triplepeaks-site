Frequently asked

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I connect Strava?

Open the Triple Peaks app and go to Settings. Tap Connect Strava and authorize access. Your rides, runs, and swims will start syncing automatically, including GPS, heart rate, and power.

How do I disconnect Strava?

To disconnect, revoke Triple Peaks' access in your Strava settings under "My Apps". Your synced data in Triple Peaks stays until you delete your account.

Is there an Android app?

Not officially yet — the iPhone app is on the App Store, and the Android app is in closed beta. Want in? Email triplepeaks@online.de and we'll send you a beta invite. In the meantime, the web app works on any device.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Open your device's subscription settings to change or cancel your plan — your training history stays safe either way.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and select Delete Account. Your account and data are removed from the app, and clear from our backups over time. You can also email triplepeaks@online.de to request deletion directly.

My activities aren't syncing

Check that your Strava connection is active in Settings. Tap Sync Profile under the Strava section to trigger a manual sync. If it still fails, revoke access in Strava's app settings and reconnect from the Triple Peaks app. If the issue persists, email us and we'll dig in.

Do you use AI, and what happens to my data?

Yes — AI coaching is the core of the app. To build your plans, analyses, and chat replies, we send your training and performance data to an AI provider. Before we send it, we remove direct identifiers like your name and email, so the provider sees your training data rather than who you are. You agree to this when you sign up, and you can turn AI features off or delete your account at any time. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

Is my data secure?

We take reasonable care: connections are encrypted, access is limited to what's needed to run the app, and we never sell your data. No app can promise perfect security, but we keep the moving parts few and use established providers. Full details live in our Privacy Policy.
