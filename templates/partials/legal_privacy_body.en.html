Triple Peaks uses your training data for one thing: to coach you. We don't sell it, we don't run ads, and we keep the list of services that touch it short. Here is the whole story.

Who runs Triple Peaks

Lukas Carullo, Switzerland. Triple Peaks is a one-person project, not a company. For anything about your data, email triplepeaks@online.de. This policy covers triplepeaks.coach, app.triplepeaks.coach, and the iOS and Android apps.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and, if you add it, your first name. You can sign in with Apple, with an email address, or with Strava. We never see your Apple or Strava password.
  • Your training: the activities and profile details you share from Strava or Apple Health, such as distance, pace, heart rate, power, and GPS.
  • What you tell the coach: your goals, availability, check-ins, feedback, and chat messages.
  • What the app creates for you: plans, analyses, and coach replies.
  • What it takes to run the app: your subscription status from Apple or Google (we never see your card details), device type, app version, and crash reports.

What we do with it

We build and adapt your plan, analyse your workouts and races, and answer your questions. That's it. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the services listed below. Connections are encrypted, backups are encrypted, and access is limited to what running the app requires.

AI coaching

Your plans, analyses, and chat replies are written by an AI model run by a third-party AI provider. Before anything is sent, we strip direct identifiers such as your name and email address, so the provider receives your training data, not your identity. Your passwords, Strava tokens, and payment details are never sent, and the provider does not use this data to train its models. What the coach says is a suggestion, not medical advice: use your own judgment, and talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme.

Services we rely on

  • An AI provider generates your plans, analyses, and chat replies (EU and US).
  • A hosting provider in the EU runs the app, the database, and backups.
  • An email provider sends account and notification emails (US).
  • A crash-reporting service collects crash reports from the apps.
  • Strava and Apple Health are connected by you, and their own privacy policies apply to those accounts.
  • Apple and Google distribute the apps and handle subscriptions on their own terms.

Some of these process data outside Switzerland and the EU, mainly in the United States, under their standard data-protection terms. Email us if you'd like to know which companies these are.

Your data, your call

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account in the app's settings at any time, or email us, and your data is removed from our systems (backups clear over time). Want to see, correct, or get a copy of your data? Email us and we'll sort it out.

This website

triplepeaks.coach sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own domain. The app uses only what it needs to keep you signed in.

The rest

Triple Peaks is meant for adults and not intended for anyone under 16. If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for anything important, let you know in the app or by email. Questions? triplepeaks@online.de.
